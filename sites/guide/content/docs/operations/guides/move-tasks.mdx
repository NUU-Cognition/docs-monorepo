---
title: "Move Tasks"
---

This guide shows you how to move work in NUU Operations. You move one task, many tasks, or a whole section. At the end the work sits where it belongs, with its subtasks.

## Prerequisites

- You have a workspace open. See [About Workspaces](/operations/concepts/about-workspaces).
- You know that a task lives in a section, and that the backlog is a section. See [About Tasks and Backlogs](/operations/concepts/about-tasks-and-backlogs).

## Open the Move dialog

Select a task in a list, or open the task. Press `M`. The Move dialog opens.

You can also open Move from the menu of a task row.

The dialog lists the places of the workspace. It opens at once, because Ops keeps a live list of those places. Type part of a name to filter the list.

## Choose a place

Choose where the task goes:

| You choose | The task goes to |
| --- | --- |
| A section | That section |
| A project | The backlog section of the project |
| A deliverable | The first open section of the deliverable. If it has no open section, the service makes one. |
| A deployment | The inbox of the deployment |
| A task | Under that task, as a subtask |

Confirm the move. The task does not copy. It moves.

## Subtasks move too

When you move a task, its subtasks move with it. The whole tree goes to the new place. You do not move subtasks one by one.

To move a subtask out of its tree, move it to a section. It becomes a root task there.

## Move many tasks

Select more than one task in a list. Press `M`. Choose one place for all of them.

Each selected task moves with its subtasks. Use this to clear a backlog or an inbox in one step.

## Move a section

Open the menu of a section. Choose Move. Choose a new home in the same project: the project, a deliverable, or a deployment.

The section keeps its tasks and their order. Use this when work that started in the project becomes part of a deliverable.

## Capture first, commit later

The backlog is the place for work that is not committed yet. Make the task there. Move it into a section of a deliverable when the work becomes part of a delivery.

A move from a backlog into another section clears the priority. Other moves keep it.

## Check the result

Open the new place. The task should be there, with the same subtask count.

If the task has an owner, that person can get a notification. If a task of yours moved, you can get a notification.

Search can find the task in the active workspace. Activity records the move.
