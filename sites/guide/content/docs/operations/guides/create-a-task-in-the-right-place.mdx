---
title: "Create a Task in the Right Place"
---

This guide shows you how to make a task from anywhere in NUU Operations and put it in the right place at once. It also shows you how to go to any place fast. At the end you can capture work without a click through the project pages.

## Prerequisites

- You have a workspace open. See [About Workspaces](/operations/concepts/about-workspaces).
- You know that a task lives in a section. See [About Tasks and Backlogs](/operations/concepts/about-tasks-and-backlogs).

## Why it is fast

Ops keeps a live list of the places of the active workspace: each project, deliverable, section, and deployment, with its title and its home. It loads the list once and keeps it up to date. Go to, quick create, and Move read that list. So they open and filter with no wait.

## Go to a place

Press Cmd+K. Go to opens.

Type part of a name. The list shows the matching projects, deliverables, sections, and deployments. Each row shows the path to the place, for example `Website / Spring launch / Checkout`. Places that you opened recently rank higher.

Choose a place. Ops opens it.

## Quick create a task

Press `C` or `N` anywhere in Ops, when the cursor is not in a text field. Quick create opens.

Type the task. Write it the way you would say it, for example `Fix the checkout total on mobile`.

As you type, Ops suggests places for the task. The best place is first. Each suggestion shows its path.

The first suggestions come at once, from a fast match of the place titles. A moment later the suggestions of the server arrive, and the list can change.

Keep the first place, or choose another one. Then create the task. Ops makes the task in that place.

If you choose a project, the task goes to the backlog section of the project. If you choose a deliverable, it goes to the first open section of the deliverable. If you choose a deployment, it goes to the inbox of the deployment.

## Smart suggestions

Quick create has a switch: Smart suggestions. It is on by default.

The server matches your text against the titles of the places, their hidden context, and the titles of their recent tasks. When Smart suggestions is on, Ops also asks a decision model to choose the best place from those matches. The model has a short time limit. If it does not answer in time, you get the text match. You never wait for it.

When it is off, Ops makes no model call. The suggestions come from the text match only.

The switch is your setting. It stays the same the next time you open quick create.

## Help the suggestions

The suggestions are only as good as the places. To help them:

- Give projects, deliverables, sections, and deployments clear titles.
- Fill in the Context field of the places that matter. Write what each one is about, in the words people use. The page does not show the context. The suggestions read it.

## Fix a wrong place

If a task lands in the wrong place, move it. Open the task and press `M`. See [Move Tasks](/operations/guides/move-tasks).
