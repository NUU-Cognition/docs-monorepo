---
title: "Use NUU Command"
---

This guide shows you how to use NUU Command. NUU Command is a one-line bar in NUU Account. You type one sentence. It makes a task in NUU Operations, or it sparks an idea in NUU Current, in the right organisation and the right place. At the end you can capture work without opening Operations.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in with your NUU account.
- You belong to at least one organisation, or you use your personal workspace. See [About Workspaces](/operations/concepts/about-workspaces).

## Open NUU Command

Open NUU Account. Go to the Command page, `/command`. You can also open the bar with its keyboard shortcut from any page of NUU Account.

The bar has one line. Type in it.

## Choose the organisation

Type `@` and the start of an organisation name, for example `@acme`. The bar lists the organisations that match. Choose one, or type the full name.

The bar then shows the organisation as a chip. Everything that you type after it is for that organisation.

Use `@personal` for your personal workspace.

As soon as you choose an organisation, NUU Command loads the places of that organisation in the background: its projects, deliverables, sections, and deployments. So the suggestions are ready when you type the rest.

## Type the sentence

Type what you want, the way you would say it. For example:

- `create a task to fix the checkout total on mobile`
- `remind me to renew the domain`
- `spark an idea: a weekly digest of the inbox`

Words such as "create a task to", "task:", and "remind me to" ask for a task. Words such as "spark an idea", "idea:", and "spark" ask for an idea. The bar takes these words out of the title.

With no such words, NUU Command decides the action from the sentence.

You can name the place at the end, for example `fix the login in Website`. When a place matches, the bar takes the name out of the title.

## Read the preview

Before you press Enter, the bar shows what it will do:

- **The action.** Create a task in Operations, or spark an idea in NUU Current.
- **The place.** For a task, the section, deliverable, project, or deployment inbox where it will go, with its path.
- **The confidence.** How sure NUU Command is about the action and the place.

Press Tab to cycle through the other places. Stop on the place that you want.

## Run it

Press Enter. NUU Command makes the task or the idea. It shows a link to the new task in Operations, or to the new spark in NUU Current.

If the task went to another place than the one in the preview, the result says where it went.

## How it decides

NUU Command matches your sentence against the places of the organisation, then asks a decision model for the action and the place. The model has a short time limit. If it does not answer in time, NUU Command uses the text match. You never wait for it.

Quick create in Operations works the same way. See [Create a Task in the Right Place](/operations/guides/create-a-task-in-the-right-place).
